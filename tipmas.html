Tipping Visualization

Tipping was facilitated through custom-made QR codes linked to Joe Coffee’s Venmo. Each scan triggered a real-time coin drop into a digital jar, visually signifying a tip. I designed and implemented both the visualization and backend logic deployed using Heroku.
